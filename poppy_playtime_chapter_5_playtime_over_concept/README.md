# Poppy Playtime Chapter 5 - Playtime Over (concept)

## Short introduction of the game
A simple and stop-motion-like 2D webgame, basically a Poppy Playtime fangame, made for fans, YouTubers and animators.
**Not for aggrevating MOB Entertainment, but for presenting a concept and an approach of a fan, which hopefully is going to be considered as a good gift for many people!**

## More detailed introduction of the game
Poppy Playtime Chapter 5 - Playtime Over (concept) is a conceptual fangame, presuming it to be a possible final chapter. In case you do wish to include segments of it in your game or animation project, feel free to do so! While this fan-based chapter seems to be simple, don't let it deceive you, or take it lightly - it has surprises at the least expectable scenarios.
Can you walk into the den of 1-0-0-6 with knowing that this is going to be a pretty hard match? Can you withstand his last hitting cards? Can you really withstand witnessing the most preserved secret of Playtime Co?

## Features of this fangame
- New hand for the grabpack 2.0, will be essential for facing 1-0-0-6.
- Mind-blowing VHS tapes and collectable notes regarding the past of Playtime Co.
- New monsters to face with, including the dreaded 1-0-0-6. 
- Simple but must-to-solve puzzles to proceed.
- Trophies to collect, can you find all of them?

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The files in the ActualGame folder are not organized into subfolders, for the reason that if they were, then the localstorage values (helperlocalstoragevalues.js) won't work the way expected! This info is for developers!

> [!NOTE]
> This game can be played via opening the index.html file, in case of downloading the entire project!
