The files having _usable at the end of their names are the ones, which are used in the code (they have transparent background).
The white backgrounded images were drawn first and are meant to be for designers and artists.
The files having _other in their names were drawn later on, because mirroring one image didn't look accurate for the grabpack hands.
Therefore, sprites for looking at the other side were drawn with white background and from them were _usable edits made.
